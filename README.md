## Overview
The Task Manager Dashboard is a lightweight, browser-based productivity tool tailored for the Japanese market. It provides a distraction-free environment to track daily objectives, prioritize tasks, and maintain focus. Designed for professionals and students alike, it ensures your data stays private and accessible directly within your browser.

## Features
*   **CRUD Operations**: Easily add, edit, and delete tasks with a single click.
*   **Priority Management**: Assign priority levels to keep your most important work front and center.
*   **Smart Filtering**: Quickly filter tasks by status or priority.
*   **Local Persistence**: Your data is saved automatically to your browser's `localStorage`—no database required.
*   **Japanese UI/UX**: Text and layouts are optimized for Japanese readability and user experience.
